Beneath a shattered sky, drifting lands and an unfinished journey await at [sr.hoshivel.com](https://sr.hoshivel.com).

*Shattered Realms* is a 2D turn-based strategy game set in an original world. Command heroes on a hex board, shape their growth and skill sets, and explore lands adrift in the void.

On the official site you can:

- Learn the world of **the Shattering** — the broken star-seal and the lands set adrift
- Explore the **four gameplay pillars** and meet the **heroes who lead the way**
- Follow chapters along the **World Tree**: Snowbound Passage, Age of Starmarks, and more to come
- Enter the battlefield directly in your browser — click to play, no download

The game remains in development, with new chapters and content still to come. Take the first step and see the world as it takes shape.
