Signing in should be a doorway into a world, not a ritual you repeat for every game.

That is what Hoshi ID is for. From one account center, you can:

- **Register once** — use the same account across Hoshivel games
- **Stay in control** — manage your profile, sign-in history and connected services in one place
- **Keep your account secure** — manage your password and active sign-ins from the same account
- **Keep data in its place** — Hoshi ID holds your account information, while each game keeps its own game data

*Shattered Realms* is the first game to use Hoshi ID. Choose "Sign in with Hoshi ID" in the game to create or load your game profile automatically.

Future Hoshivel games will join it over time, so the same account can carry you into more worlds.
