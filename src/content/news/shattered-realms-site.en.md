The official website of *Shattered Realms* is now public at [sr.oha.li](https://sr.oha.li).

Shattered Realms is a 2D turn-based strategy game set in an original world: on fragmented lands adrift in the void, board-game tactics, RPG growth, MOBA-style skills and open-world exploration fuse on a single hex board.

On the official site you can:

- Learn the world of **the Shattering** — the broken star-seal and the lands set adrift;
- Take in the **four gameplay pillars** and the **heroes who lead**;
- Explore chapters along the **World Tree**: Snowbound Passage, Age of Starmarks, and more to grow;
- Enter the battlefield straight from the browser via **region-routed nodes** — click and play, no download.

The game is still in development, and its chapters keep growing. Come take a walk beneath the shattered sky — the unfinished journey begins.
